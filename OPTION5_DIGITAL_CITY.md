# ALIF LABS — Digital City Edition

Replaces the Option 4 liquid-metal artwork with an animated glass-skyscraper city, including perspective-projected tower geometry, lit glass windows, animated orbital platform and central illuminated landmark. No font-based city silhouette or decorative dot substitution.

Dark mode remains default, with light mode toggle. Existing client-branding, navigation, authentication and backend code are retained from the supplied Option 4 ZIP.

The scene is an original realtime canvas illustration rather than a photorealistic 3D render. It does not require additional npm dependencies.
